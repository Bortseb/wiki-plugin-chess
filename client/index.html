Attributions and Thanks

Federated Wiki Chess itself is licensed under the MIT License. It is built on free and open source software. Thanks to the following projects for their work:

  • Bootstrap · MIT

    Responsive layout, modals, buttons, and form controls used across the chess shell, dialogs, and position editor.

  • bootstrap-auto-dark-mode · MIT

    Follows the OS light/dark preference for Bootstrap’s data-bs-theme attribute.

  • jQuery (jquery-3.3.1.slim.min.js) · MIT

    Legacy DOM helper required by Bootstrap’s JavaScript bundle in the chess app shell.

  • es-module-shims · MIT

    Import-map and ES module compatibility shim for loading the chess app in older browsers.

  • chess-console · MIT

    In-browser chess game framework: board host, move history, captured pieces, persistence, and the control toolbar.

  • chess-console-stockfish · MIT

    Plugs Stockfish into chess-console: engine moves, level selection, evaluation display, and the new-game dialog.

  • chessmail.eu

    Reference deployment of browser chess with Stockfish; early inspiration for playing engine games on the web.

  • cm-chessboard · MIT

    Interactive SVG chessboard with drag-and-drop moves, legal-move hints, orientation flip, and promotion dialog.

  • cm-engine-runner · MIT

    Talks to UCI chess engines inside a Web Worker and returns best moves, scores, and search status to the UI.

  • cm-fen-editor · MIT

    Set up custom positions from FEN before starting a game; place, remove, and drag pieces on an editable board.

  • Font Awesome · CC BY 4.0 / MIT

    Toolbar, undo, flip, and copy-button icons plus modal affordances used throughout the chess interface.

  • chess_console_sounds.mp3 · CC BY 4.0

    Move, capture, and check sound effects from the chess-console asset pack, played during live play.

  • stockfish.js (stockfish-18-lite-single.js) · GPL-3.0

    Stockfish 18 compiled to WebAssembly (lite, single-threaded — no cross-origin isolation needed); runs as the computer opponent in engine games inside a dedicated worker.
